Q2)
1. 
-Straight-through
-Both ends use the same standard (e.g., both T568B). Pins connect 1-to-1.
-Connecting dissimilar devices: PC to Switch, Switch to Router, or Router to Cable/DSL modem.

2.
-Crossover
-Transmit and receive pairs are swapped at one end (Pin 1 to Pin 3, Pin 2 to Pin 6).
-Connecting similar devices: PC to PC, Switch to Switch, or Router to Router.

3.
-Rollover
-The pinout is completely reversed (Pin 1 connects to Pin 8 at the other end).
-Console access: Connecting a PC's serial port to the console port of a router or switch for configuration.
